# FirstPass

<img src="../assets/firstpass-icon.png" width="96" align="right" alt="">

**A reference mix from a folder of stems, in minutes.**

Point FirstPass at a folder of stems and it works out what each track is (kick, snare,
bass, lead vocal…), prepares it (polarity between related mics, gating, high-pass, EQ,
compression, level and pan) and gives you a balanced mix on a virtual console that you
can adjust, bounce, or carry on in your DAW.

- **Analyse:** loudness, dynamics, noise floor and spectrum of every track; the song's
  tempo and key.
- **Classify:** each track's role, from its name or its sound.
- **Prepare:** every track set up the way you'd start it yourself, with the reason for each
  decision shown on its strip.
- **Balance:** bus and master compression, the master level at your loudness target, and a
  limiter.

Then adjust anything on the console, A/B against a reference mix, and **bounce** a 24-bit
WAV, or export the whole mix to **REAPER** or **Pro Tools** and keep going there.
